🎮 STEAM GAMES ANALYSIS

Interactive Data Visualization Prototype V1

Visualization 1: Pac-Man Interactive Scatter Plot

Play as Pac-Man and explore Steam games! Each dot is a game - X is price, Y is positive review percentage. Pink dots are Action games, green ones are Indie. Stats show up on the right when you collect games.
Arrow keys or WASD to move.

Visualization 2: Peak Battle - Indie vs Studio

Strike cards represent studio games in different price ranges, while the Defend card represents all indie games. The number below each card shows how many games fall into that category. Click any Strike card to attack the Defend block!
Click Strike cards to attack.

Visualization 3: Indie vs AAA

Compare indie and AAA games on different metrics. Hover over the icons around the radar charts to see stats. Use the year buttons at the bottom to filter by release year.

Visualization 4: Game Lobby

Multiplayer vs non-multiplayer games. Hover over the left side to see more details.

Visualization 5: Space Chart

Navigate through a galaxy of games using arrow keys. Games are clustered by genre, node size is player count, opacity is positive reviews. Pink nodes are studio games, teal nodes are indie games. Fly close to games to see details.
Arrow keys to move (hover over visualization first).
