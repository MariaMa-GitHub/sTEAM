Click pink Strike cards to attack green Defend card!
🎮 HOW TO READ THIS
Pink Strike Cards: Studio games at different price points. Each card's damage = number of studio games in that price range.
Green Defend Card: All indie games combined. Block strength = total indie games.
The Peak (⛰️): Arrow points to the price range with the MOST studio games.
Insight: This shows which price ranges dominate for studio games vs. the collective strength of all indie games.
